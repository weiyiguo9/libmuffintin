q_index metadata fix (incremental)
----------------------------------
PairBlock::new after the L+S sum previously passed q_index=spin, so the
second spin block was mislabeled q=1. Both spin blocks now use q_index=0.
Spin remains the block-loop identity (orbitals, evaluate_pair_block
inputs, log messages). Matrix values, candidates, weights, thresholds,
layout, n_keep, and reporting are unchanged. Prior
ml0_adaptive_rank_probe* outputs are kept as provenance.

Method (cheapest controlled A/B)
--------------------------------
- Read NUF1 v2 adaptive dump. Preserve bando=13 vs nband=16 strides.
- Pair density is conj(L_i) L_j + conj(S_i) S_j via two public
  evaluate_pair_block calls plus PairBlock::new on the summed values.
- Semantic PairColumnLayout::new(nk, nband, None) with layout.encode(k, i, j).
  n_k=27, n_orb=16, n_columns = 27 * 16 * 16 = 6912 per spin.
- q = 0 only (KMesh::gamma_centred([3,3,3], |a1|), iq=0). Umklapp is
  identically 1; this is not an Umklapp / all-q claim.
- Both spins stacked as two PairBlocks into public pivots_from_pair_blocks.
- Candidate subsets: independent deterministic region-strided samples at
  M=512 and M=2048, allocating floor(M * n_mt / npts) muffin-tin indices
  from the dump prefix and the remainder from the interstitial suffix.
  Same subset is reused for the true-weight and uniform-weight arms.
- True weights: dump quadrature on the candidate points, no clamp/floor.
- Uniform weights: the mean of those true candidate weights (global scale
  does not change relative QR ranks; this only matches R_00 magnitude).
- n_keep = M (>= 512) so threshold ranks cannot silently stop at 128.
- Rank from QRCP |R_kk| / |R_00| at 1e-1, 3e-2, 1e-2, 3e-3.

Gate (no tighter physics tolerance)
-----------------------------------
Pass only if, on both M:
  1. true-weight rank at 3e-3 is strictly below n_keep (not saturated)
  2. true-weight ranks do not sharply diverge from the matched uniform arm
  3. 3e-3 pivots do not collapse onto near-minimum-weight candidates
Compare the M=512 uniform arm to the recorded 113-at-3e-3 number, and
explain candidate / pair-space / n_keep differences rather than forcing
equality.
